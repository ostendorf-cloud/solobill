- All six Zeilen and Kennzahlen were printed clearly. None is marked not verified.
- The form prints a minus at Kz 142 (Zeile 72) and a plus at Kz 176 (Zeile 73). The keys carry
  no sign. The EÜR report (#80) applies it.
- Kind `expense` for all six: the owner enters them. Computing Zeilen 71 to 73 (Fahrtenbuch,
  1 percent rule, Entfernungspauschale) stays out of the milestone.
- Known effect until #80 applies the sign: Zeile 72 is a deduction and Zeile 73 a computed allowance,
  not payments. An expense booked with `euer.z72` is added to the totals of the expense list, and
  both Zeilen are always flagged `missing_receipt`, because they have no receipt. #80 decides how
  they are booked and whether they leave the flag.
