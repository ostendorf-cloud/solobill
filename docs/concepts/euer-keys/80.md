## Zeilen 72 and 73, the sign

- The form prints a minus at Kz 142 (Zeile 72) and a plus at Kz 176 (Zeile 73). Both fields take
  a positive amount. The operator says how the line enters the sum of Zeile 75.
- Mapping: the row of `euer.z72` carries `"operator": "-"`, the row of `euer.z73`
  `"operator": "+"`. The report shows the operator next to the Kennzahl and keeps the amount
  positive. It computes no sum of the expenses.
- Booking: the owner books both as an expense, with the amount as it goes into the field, no
  VAT, and the paid_on date inside the year they belong to, for example 31 December.
- Missing receipt: the report raises no `missing_receipt` warning for either key. Both are
  computed figures, not payments, and have no receipt. The expense JSON still flags them, as
  `76.md` describes: `storage.Expense.MissingReceipt` is owned by #76 and is not changed here.
- Owner question: should the expense list stop flagging them too?

## Missing receipt and storno

- A storno copies the file of its original. Both rows of a pair without a receipt carry the same
  missing receipt.
- The report raises one `missing_receipt` warning per original. Its `related_ids` list every
  storno of that original counted in the year. A storno whose original counts in another year
  raises its own warning, with the original in `related_ids`.

## Storno and reversal

- A payment reversal undoes a recording mistake. It follows the year and the regime of the
  payment it reverses.
- A storno of an expense or other income is money flowing back on its own date. It counts in the
  year of that date, or of its own assignment, not in the year of its original.
- Its amounts negate a split made under the regime of the original, so it takes that regime and
  leaves the same lines: net and Zeile 57 or 17 for a Regelbesteuerung original, gross for a
  Kleinunternehmer original, even after a switch.
- Not verified: this reading of section 11 EStG and section 17 UStG.
- Steuerberater question: "An expense paid under the Regelbesteuerung is refunded after the
  switch to the Kleinunternehmerregelung. Does the refund reduce the expense net and the
  Vorsteuer of Zeile 57 in the year of the refund, or something else?"

## Readings not verified, open for the owner

Both are what the report computes today. Neither is decided by solobill as correct.

- A refund after a correction that lowers only one VAT line. The refund takes the ratio of the
  corrected document as a whole, so its VAT share is spread pro rata over every bucket. Example
  from the independent review of #80, not recomputed here: Zeile 17 shows 16.51 VAT, while the
  corrected invoice itself carries 15.20 VAT.
- Steuerberater question: "An invoice with a 19 percent and a 7 percent line is corrected by
  lowering only the 19 percent line, and the difference is refunded. Is the VAT of the refund
  the full 19 percent share of the lowered amount, or a share pro rata over both rates?"
- A Kleinunternehmer invoice paid after the switch to the Regelbesteuerung. The payment counts by
  the regime of its date, so it lands on Zeile 15 with 0.00 VAT on Zeile 17.
- Steuerberater question: "An invoice issued as a Kleinunternehmer without VAT is paid after the
  switch to the Regelbesteuerung. Does the receipt belong on Zeile 12, on Zeile 15 with no VAT,
  or on another line?"
