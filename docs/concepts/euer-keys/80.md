# EÜR line keys of the report (#80)

The keys `internal/category/keys_80.go` registers. Only #80 edits this file after #67.

The line mapping per tax year is `internal/euer/mapping/<year>.json`. This file records how
the three open points of the contract are handled there. None of them could be checked against
the form: the form was not at hand while #80 was built. Each one is the conservative choice,
marked `"verified": false` in the mapping data, and an open question for the owner.

## Zeile 65, Kz 162 or 172

- Not verified: which of Kz 162 and 172 is the deductible part of the häusliches Arbeitszimmer.
- Choice: the mapping shows no Kennzahl for either key. The report shows Zeile 65 and the
  amount, with an empty Kennzahl, rather than a guessed one.
- Owner question: which Kennzahl belongs to the non deductible and which to the deductible
  part? Then fill `kennzahl` in both rows of the 2025 mapping and set `verified`.

## Zeile 13

- Not verified: whether Zeile 13 (nicht steuerbare Umsätze eines Kleinunternehmers, Kz 119 in
  the plan) is a line the owner chooses or one the report derives.
- Choice: no key for Zeile 13. Every receipt under the Kleinunternehmer regime goes to Zeile
  12. Nothing in solobill marks a receipt as nicht steuerbar today, so no rule could derive it.
- Owner question: does any receipt belong on Zeile 13, for example a service to a customer
  abroad? If yes, Zeile 13 becomes a chosen key here, with a row below.

## Kz 111 on Zeile 10 or Zeile 12

- Not verified: the read copy prints 111 next to Zeile 10 as well as on Zeile 12.
- Choice: `euer.z12` keeps Kz 111, as `67.md` registered it, and its mapping row is marked
  unverified, so the report flags the Kennzahl. Zeile 10 has no key.
- Owner question: which line does Kz 111 belong to? If it is Zeile 10, the mapping row of
  `euer.z12` gets the Kennzahl of Zeile 12 instead.
