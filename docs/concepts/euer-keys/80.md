# EÜR line keys of the report (#80)

The keys `internal/category/keys_80.go` registers. Only #80 edits this file after #67.

The line mapping per tax year is `internal/euer/mapping/<year>.json`. This file records how the
three open points of the contract and the sign of the car lines are handled there.

## Source

- BMF letter of 29 August 2025, GZ IV C 6 - S 2142/00023/010/001, "Standardisierte
  Einnahmenüberschussrechung nach § 60 Absatz 4 EStDV; Anlage EÜR 2025".
- Read as the copy
  <https://www.steuerschroeder.de/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-08-29-anlage-EUER-2025.pdf>
  on 25 November 2026, the same copy `76.md` read. The text was extracted with `pdftotext
  -layout` (poppler 26.11.0) and compared line by line with every row of the 2025 mapping.
- The BMF host answered 503 on 25 November 2026 and was not read.
- Every Zeile and Kennzahl of the 2025 mapping matches the extracted text. The 2025 mapping is
  `verified`. The 2026 mapping is a copy, `unverified` until the owner compares it with the 2026
  form.
- Not tax advice. Which entry belongs on which line stays the owner's decision.

## Zeile 65, Kz 162 and 172

- Settled by the form: Zeile 65 has two amount columns, headed "nicht abziehbar" and
  "abziehbar". Kz 162 stands in the first, Kz 172 in the second.
- The same layout gives Zeile 62 (164, 174) and Zeile 63 (165, 175), which `67.md` already
  registered in that order.
- Mapping: `euer.z65.nd` is Kz 162, `euer.z65.d` is Kz 172, both verified.
- Caveat: the reading rests on the column position in the extracted text. The owner may check it
  once against the printed form.

## Zeile 13

- The form text: "davon nicht steuerbare Umsätze sowie Umsätze nach § 19 Abs. 2 Satz 1 Nr. 1
  und 2 sowie Satz 2 UStG", Kz 119, indented under Zeile 12.
- Settled by the form: Zeile 13 is a "davon" line. Its amount is part of Zeile 12, not added to
  it. Leaving it empty does not change the sum of the receipts.
- Not settled: whether any receipt of the owner belongs on it. Nothing in solobill marks a receipt
  as nicht steuerbar or as a turnover under section 19 Abs. 2 UStG, so no rule could derive it.
- Choice: no key for Zeile 13. Every receipt under the Kleinunternehmer regime goes to Zeile 12.
- Owner question: does any receipt belong on Zeile 13, for example a service to a customer
  abroad? If yes, Zeile 13 needs a marker on the receipt and a key here.

## Kz 111 on Zeile 10 and Zeile 12

- The form prints 111 twice. On Zeile 10 it is the code field of the question "Wurde im
  Kalenderjahr/Wirtschaftsjahr der Betrieb beendet?", answered 1 or 2. On Zeile 12 it is the
  amount field in the section "1. Betriebseinnahmen".
- Settled by the form: the amount of the Kleinunternehmer receipts goes into the field of
  Zeile 12, Kz 111. `euer.z12` keeps Kz 111, as `67.md` registered it, now verified.
- Zeile 10 is no amount and gets no key. Why both fields print 111 is not explained by the form
  text. The ELSTER field numbers may differ, which is not verified.

## Zeilen 72 and 73, the sign

- The form prints a minus at Kz 142 (Zeile 72) and a plus at Kz 176 (Zeile 73). Both fields take
  a positive amount. The operator says how the line enters the sum of Zeile 75.
- Mapping: the row of `euer.z72` carries `"operator": "-"`, the row of `euer.z73`
  `"operator": "+"`. The report shows the operator next to the Kennzahl and keeps the amount
  positive. It computes no sum of the expenses.
- Booking: the owner books both as an expense, with the amount as it goes into the field, no
  VAT, and the paid_on date inside the year they belong to, for example 31 December.
- Missing receipt: the report raises no `missing_receipt` warning for either key. Both are
  computed figures, not payments, and have no receipt. The expense JSON still flags them, as
  `76.md` describes: `storage.Expense.MissingReceipt` is owned by #76 and is not changed here.
- Owner question: should the expense list stop flagging them too?

## Missing receipt and storno

- A storno copies the file of its original. Both rows of a pair without a receipt carry the same
  missing receipt.
- The report raises one `missing_receipt` warning per original. Its `related_ids` list every
  storno of that original counted in the year. A storno whose original counts in another year
  raises its own warning, with the original in `related_ids`.
